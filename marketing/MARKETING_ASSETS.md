## Long Description (App Store / Play Store)

**Ever wonder who that person was?**

You locked eyes at a concert. Shared a smile at a baseball game. Sat near each other at a coffee shop. But you never said hi.

**Hi Again changes that.**

We help you discover people who were in the same place at the same time as you. Whether it's a Taylor Swift concert, a local brewery, or your morning commute - Hi Again finds the moments you shared with others.

**How it works:**
1. Add locations you've visited (or import your Google Timeline)
2. Our algorithm matches you with others who were there too
3. Send a connection request and start a conversation
4. Turn missed connections into real relationships

**Features:**
- **Path Crossing Detection** - Find people from past events, concerts, and everyday places
- **Real-time GPS Matching** - See who's nearby right now
- **Missed Connections** - Post about someone you're hoping to find
- **Premium VIP** - See who viewed your profile, unlimited messages, verified badge
- **Photo & Video Feed** - Share moments with people you've crossed paths with
- **Privacy First** - You control your location data, delete anytime

**Perfect for:**
- Concert & festival goers
- Sports fans
- Regular coffee shop visitors
- Anyone who believes in serendipity

Download Hi Again and say hello to the connections you almost missed.
